Britain, 510 AD

Setting · 510 AD

A kingdom without a king

Britain has suffered years of division. Powerful lords rule their own domains, old alliances are fragile, and foreign enemies test the borders. Yet the land is not merely political: ancient forests, forgotten Roman places, saints, prophecies, strange creatures and old stories remain very real.

Your characters arrive at a moment when the future is still uncertain. They are not spectators to the legend—they are people living inside it.

“What kind of knight will you become when loyalty, honor and desire no longer point in the same direction?”
